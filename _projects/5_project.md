My work in this area examines cybercrime and cyber deviance, including online harassment, cyberbullying, digital piracy, identity theft, and other forms of technology-facilitated offending and victimization.

I am particularly interested in how individual characteristics, social environments, moral beliefs, opportunity, and emerging technologies shape cyber offending and victimization.

My broader interests also include cyberterrorism, cybersecurity, and threats to critical infrastructure, particularly how governments and criminal justice agencies prepare for, prevent, and respond to technology-based security threats.
