My research in this area examines crime and criminal justice from comparative and cross-national perspectives.

I am particularly interested in understanding how social, cultural, legal, and institutional contexts shape crime, victimization, public attitudes, and criminal justice practices across countries.

Much of my work draws on research from the United States and South Korea, while also considering broader international comparisons to better understand both similarities and differences in crime and justice.
