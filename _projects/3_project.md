My research in this area examines how people perceive police, criminal justice institutions, and the legitimacy and effectiveness of the justice system.

I am particularly interested in how media exposure, personal experiences, social background, and institutional practices shape attitudes toward police and criminal justice.

My work also examines organizational issues within criminal justice agencies, including the experiences of police and correctional officers, workplace stress, victimization, and organizational outcomes.
