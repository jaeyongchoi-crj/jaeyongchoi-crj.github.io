My work in this area focuses on terrorism, homeland security, and the policies and institutions developed to prevent and respond to security threats.

I am particularly interested in the causes and development of terrorism, counterterrorism policy, homeland security institutions, and the ways governments balance security, law, and public safety.

This area also reflects my broader interest in comparative approaches to terrorism and security, including how different countries organize and respond to contemporary threats.
