My research in this area examines how immigration, acculturation, and multicultural family backgrounds shape experiences with crime and justice across generations.

I am particularly interested in immigrants' perceptions of crime, police, and the justice system, as well as the experiences and developmental outcomes of children and adolescents from multicultural families.

This research also considers how cultural adaptation, family background, victimization, and social environments influence attitudes, behavior, and well-being.
